Pest Control

This website promotes kaitiakitanga by letting New Zealanders know about pest control and how invasive species affect our environment. By learning about pest and taking action, communities can help protect our native wildlife and ecosystems

Example of Pests in NZ

Rats, Stoats, Possums, Feral cats, and Hedgehogs are some of New Zealand's most harmful pests, affecting our environment.
